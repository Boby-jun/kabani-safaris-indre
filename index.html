How to sign in

  1. Tap Continue with your email.
  2. Type the email address you gave us when you booked.
  3. We send you an 8-digit code. It usually arrives within a minute. If you can't see it, check your spam or junk folder.
  4. Type the code. It signs you in by itself.

There is no password. You get a new code each time you sign in, and you are signed out after 15 minutes without using the page.
